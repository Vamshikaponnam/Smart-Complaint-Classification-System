---

## 🚀 Quick Start (Windows)

1. Double-click the **`start_all.bat`** script in the root folder.
2. Wait for both servers to initialize.
3. Open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) in your browser.

---

## 🐳 Docker Deployment

1. Ensure **Docker Desktop** is running.
2. Run `docker-compose up -d --build` in the root folder.
3. Access the UI at `http://localhost:3000`.

## ✨ Elite AI Features (Updated)

### 👁️ Multi-modal Vision AI
- **Infrastructure Defect Detection**: Automatically identifies potholes, leaks, and hazards from images.
- **Integrity Scoring**: AI verifies reports to prevent spam and prioritize genuine issues.

### 📡 Sovereign Strategic Dispatch
- **Autonomous Handoff**: High-priority complaints are dispatched to department heads and external vendors.
- **Departmental Intelligence**: Real-time analytics on resolution efficiency and satisfaction.

### 🔮 Predictive Municipal Forecasting
- **Risk Analysis**: Predicts infrastructure risks 30 days in advance using historical data.
- **Strategic Insights**: Generates governance reports for municipal authorities.

### 🌍 Universal Accessibility
- **Real-time Translation**: Automatic detection and translation for multi-lingual citizen support.
- **Glassmorphic AI Chatbot**: Premium responsive assistant with local-only fallback.

### ⚖️ Governance & Sustainability
- **Zonal Health Monitoring**: Interactive health scores for different city sectors.
- **Sustainability Impact**: Calculates CO2 savings and environmental benefits.
